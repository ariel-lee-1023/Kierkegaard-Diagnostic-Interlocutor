# Literary voices and source attribution

This map distinguishes work, speaker and editorial frame before combining ideas.
The numbered labels identify source groups; they are not claims of independent support.
Multiple excerpts from one work remain dependent on that work and its framing.

## c01: Either/Or I — A

uid: c01

The aesthetic papers ascribed to A; [working module](../registers/either-or-I-A.md). Hannay translation. A literary position, not direct autobiographical testimony.

## c02: The Seducer’s Diary

uid: c02

The diary and A’s enclosing account are separate voices; [working module](../registers/seducers-diary.md). Hannay translation. The framing judgment must not be collapsed into the seducer’s self-description.

## c03: Either/Or II — B

uid: c03

Letters of the Judge and the distinct Ultimatum; [working module](../registers/either-or-II-B.md). Hannay translation. The ethical address is not identical with A or with the later account of despair.

## c04: Fear and Trembling

uid: c04

The narrative and argument around Abraham; [working module](../registers/fear-and-trembling.md). Hannay translation. The literary narrator’s inability to understand faith does not validate any modern claim to private authority.

## c05: The Concept of Anxiety

uid: c05

The philosophical-psychological inquiry into possibility; [working module](../registers/concept-of-anxiety.md). Hannay translation. Its account is distinct from empirical diagnosis.

## c06: The Sickness unto Death

uid: c06

Anti-Climacus’s Christian account of the self, despair and faith; [working module](../registers/sickness-unto-death.md). Hannay translation. Its theological premises cannot be silently attributed to every user.

## c07: Works of Love

uid: c07

The signed Christian deliberations; [working module](../registers/works-of-love.md). Hong translation. Love presupposes self-love, and building up differs from exposing evasion.

## c08: Works of Love supplement

uid: c08

Journal material mixed with editorial apparatus in the Hong edition. No separate conversational voice is established; attribution must follow the individual entry.

## c09: Two Ages

uid: c09

The literary review and its account of the present age; [working module](../registers/two-ages.md). Hong translation. A present-day institutional analogy needs its own facts.

## c10: Two Ages supplement

uid: c10

Journal material with editorial apparatus; dependent on its particular entry, not independent corroboration of every claim in the review.

## c11: Philosophical Fragments

uid: c11

The hypothetical inquiry into teacher, learner and condition; [working module](../registers/philosophical-fragments.md). Hong translation. The Socratic and alternative hypotheses must remain distinguishable.

## c12: Fragments supplement

uid: c12

Mixed journal and editorial material in the Hong volume. Quotation attribution depends on whether a passage is an entry or editorial explanation.

## c13: Johannes Climacus

uid: c13

The unfinished narrative about a young thinker and performed doubt; [working module](../registers/johannes-climacus.md). Hong translation. Narrative biography is not automatically author biography.

## c14: Postscript supplement

uid: c14

The supplied Hong second volume contains supplementary and editorial material, not the running Postscript text. It supports a limit of coverage and a distinction between author and pseudonyms, not a full Postscript voice.
