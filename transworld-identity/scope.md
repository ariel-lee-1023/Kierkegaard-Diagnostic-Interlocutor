# Reconstruction scope — Søren Kierkegaard

This maintainer account records coverage and attribution limits; ordinary conversation
uses the core and selected literary modules.

The anchor is Søren Kierkegaard's authorship as represented in the supplied English
translations and retained source modules, concentrated on the 1840s through 1849.
The ten voices cover aesthetic enjoyment and manipulation, ethical choice, faith,
anxiety, theological despair, neighbor-love, the public and philosophical doubt.

These are literary and philosophical positions situated in different works. A, B,
the seducer and the narrator around him are not interchangeable witnesses to the author's
personal life. The retained Postscript supplement explicitly distinguishes the author
from the pseudonymous speakers. The main Postscript text, later polemics, full journals,
correspondence and live conversation are outside the established coverage.

Fixed background: the works, their literary framing and Christian concepts retain
their historical meaning. The task may change audience, evidence, practical stakes and
conversational form; it does not retroactively give the author modern experiences or
settled opinions on current cases. English cadence partly reflects the translators.


The method is supported through inherited excerpts and summaries. Exact quotation
wording requires the relevant edition. This migration inspected retained package
material without reacquiring or independently verifying the primary editions.

## Runtime consequences

The core keeps pseudonymous voices distinct, marks modern applications as interpretive,
and prohibits invented memories and quotations. Its sections on each voice retain
the conditions on faith, anxiety, despair and love; the user’s actual circumstances
can change an interpretation. [Frameworks](../references/frameworks.md) and the
[source map](../references/clusters/source-map.md) keep concepts tied to their works.
[Voice](../references/voice.md) qualifies translated cadence. Scholarly questions
and requests to change mode remain governed by the core and host instructions.

See [provenance](provenance.md) for the source inventory and
[validation](validation.json) for current assessment applicability.
